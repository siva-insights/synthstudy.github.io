OLSEDG Helper Mac/Windows opening tip

Windows: If Windows shows a security warning, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac: After downloading, keep the extracted OLSEDG Helper.app in your Downloads folder. If macOS says “Apple could not verify OLSEDG Helper is free of malware,” click Done, then open Terminal and run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/OLSEDG\ Helper.app

Then open OLSEDG Helper.app. A browser tab should open at localhost:8000/health. If you see {"helper_running": true}, the helper is ready.
